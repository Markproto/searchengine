Proof-of-work challenge gates all document download endpoints (Epstein, Climate, WEF, Archive). Real browsers solve a SHA-256 puzzle in ~250ms (at K=18) and get a 1-hour, 50-download session token. AI crawlers (GPTBot, Amazonbot, etc.) get auto-403 before the puzzle is even shown. curl/wget/scripts can't solve the puzzle.
