How It Works

  1. Sitemap Discovery — Scans robots.txt and common sitemap locations to find the website's sitemap files.
  2. URL Filtering — Parses sitemaps and filters URLs by the lastmod date within your chosen range.
  3. Content Extraction — Fetches each page, extracts the article title, content, author, and publication date.
  4. Indexing — Indexes articles into Elasticsearch, skipping any that are already in the index.

Note: Not all websites have sitemaps, and some sitemaps may not include date information. Results depend on the website's sitemap quality. The crawler respects a polite delay between requests.
