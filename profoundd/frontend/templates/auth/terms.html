Profoundd AI Analysis Service

1. Service Description

Profoundd provides AI-powered article analysis including sentiment analysis, bias detection, key point extraction, and prediction market comparison. This service uses artificial intelligence models from third-party providers (Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI) to generate analysis.

2. Account & API Keys

Your first 5 AI analyses are provided free using Profoundd's resources. After that, you must provide your own API key from a supported provider (Claude/Anthropic, ChatGPT/OpenAI, or Grok/xAI). Your API key is stored with encryption and is used solely to process your own analysis requests. Profoundd does not share your API key with any third party beyond the selected AI provider.

3. Data Collection & Usage

When you use the AI analysis feature, Profoundd stores:

  • The article URL, title, and summary analyzed
  • The AI-generated analysis text and metadata (sentiment, bias notes)
  • Your account information (email, usage count)

This data may be used to improve the service, generate aggregate insights, and train future analysis features. Individual analysis data is not shared publicly but may be used in anonymized, aggregate form for research purposes.
